The idea

What makes this league unique

friendly volley ball leage is a unique and exciting friendly tournament where players from our regular volleyball teams are selected through a live auction to form brand-new teams. This format encourages teamwork, sportsmanship, and healthy competition by bringing together players who don't usually play on the same side.

The league follows a double round-robin format, with every team playing each opponent twice during the league stage. Teams earn points based on match results, and at the end of the league stage, the top four teams qualify for the semifinals while the fifth-placed team is eliminated.

The tournament concludes with the semifinals and grand final to determine the Auction League Champions.

Season path

From sign-up to final whistle

Five stages, in order. Nothing about the schedule is decided until the teams are.

Step 1

Registration

Players register individually and choose their registration option: $50 (includes indoor court booking and winner/runner prize money) or $30 (indoor court booking only).

Step 2

Auction night

Five team captains draft their rosters from the full player pool. Each team ends up with 8 players.

Step 3

Double round robin

Each team plays every other team twice during league stage matches.

Step 4

League standings

After league stage points are finalized, top four teams qualify for semifinals and the fifth-placed team is eliminated.

Step 5

Semifinals & grand final

The tournament ends with semifinals and a grand final to crown the Auction League Champions.

On court

How matches are actually played

The format is built to guarantee everyone gets real game time — and to keep matches moving.

25

Scoring format

Every league match is a single set, played to 25 points.

If the set reaches 24–24 (deuce), play continues — but the deuce win-by-2 rule is capped: whichever team reaches 30 points first wins outright, regardless of margin.

8

Squad size & game time

Each team has 8 registered players for the season.

Every player on the roster must play a minimum of 3 matches across the season — captains have to rotate the squad, not just run the same six.

Fair playing-time rule
6+2

Match-day squad

Before each match, the team captain names a starting six and two substitutes from the 8-player roster.

Only those 8 are eligible to play that match.

!

No early substitutions

To make sure everyone gets a fair chance, the captain's starting top 6 must begin the match and stay on court for the first 10 combined points of the set.

No substitution is allowed during those first 10 points (including for injury). If a player cannot continue in that period, the team continues short-handed. Substitutions are allowed only after the set passes 10 combined points.

Applies even in case of injury
