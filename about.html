The idea

What makes this league unique

Volleyball Premier League is a unique and exciting tournament where players from our regular volleyball teams are selected through a live auction to form brand-new teams. This format encourages teamwork, sportsmanship, and healthy competition by bringing together players who don't usually play on the same side.

The league follows a double round-robin format, with every team playing each opponent twice during the league stage. Teams earn points based on match results, and at the end of the league stage, the top four teams qualify for the semifinals while the fifth-placed team is eliminated.

The tournament concludes with the semifinals and Grand Final to determine the Auction League Champions.

Season path

From sign-up to final whistle

Five stages, in order. Nothing about the schedule is decided until the teams are.

Step 1

Registration

Players register individually and choose their registration option: $50 (includes indoor court booking and winner/runner prize money) or $30 (indoor court booking only).

Step 2

Auction night

Five owners draft their rosters from the full player pool. Each team ends up with 8 players (owner included).

Step 3

Double round robin

Each team plays every other team twice during the league stage.

Step 4

League standings

After league points are finalised, the top four teams qualify for the semifinals and the fifth-placed team is eliminated.

Step 5

Semifinals & Grand Final

The tournament ends with the semifinals and a Grand Final to crown the Auction League Champions.
