How ranking & tie-breaks work
  1. Points — 3 for a win, 0 for a loss (league matches are a single set).
  2. Wins
  3. Head-to-head — who won when the level teams played each other.
  4. Head-to-head point difference — margin in those meetings.
  5. Overall point difference — points for − points against.
  6. Points scored — total points for.
  7. ⚠️ Committee decision — if teams are still exactly level on all of the above, the organising committee decides the final order.
