Auction Rules & Playbook

Everything for auction night — the budget, base prices, how bidding works, how teams are balanced, and a step-by-step guide to running it from the console.

Owners & teams

Owners are drawn — and they play

🎟️

Team by chit

On auction day the 5 owners each draw a team chit — Ultra Eagles, Nitro Kangaroos, Ignite Emus, Thunder Tigers or Young Yaks. That decides which team they own.

👤

Owner = player

Each owner is part of their own team and counts as one of its players. So every team is the owner plus the players they win at auction.

💰

$100k each

Every team starts with an equal purse of $100,000 to build its squad. The owner joins at no cost to the purse.

🎲

Nomination by chit

All remaining players' chits go into a draw. A chit is pulled → that player goes “on the block” → all five owners bid.

Base prices & bidding

How bidding works

$15k

Spiker base price

Spikers — Outside Hitters and Opposite / Right-Side attackers — start at $15,000.

16 spikers in the pool
$5k

Everyone else

Setters, Middle Blockers, Liberos and Defensive Specialists start at $5,000.

+$1k

Bid increments

Bids rise in minimum steps of $1,000. Highest bid when the hammer falls wins the player at that price.

🛡️

Max-bid safety cap

An owner can never bid so much that they can't finish a legal squad. Their max bid is capped so every remaining slot can still be filled at base price.

Shown live in the console

Max-bid — worked example

A team needs 4 more players (including the one on the block) and has $60,000 left. After winning this player, 3 slots remain — each must keep at least the $5,000 base in reserve, i.e. 3 × $5,000 = $15,000 held back.

Max bid = $60,000 − $15,000 = $45,000

The console works this out automatically for every team, so the auctioneer can see each owner's ceiling at a glance.

Squad size & balancing

Filling the teams fairly

We're aiming for even squads. Exact numbers depend on final registrations — the rules below keep it balanced no matter what.

Target

7 or 8 per team (owner included)

Ideally teams are equal. With the current pool that means owner + 6 or owner + 7 — roughly 8 / 8 / 8 / 7 / 7. We're expecting 2 more registrations to even things out.

Odd numbers

Extra player → team with the most budget left

If the pool doesn't divide evenly, any surplus player is placed with the team that has the highest remaining budget — so the team that spent least picks up the extra body.

Juniors

Junior players

Junior players may be placed directly with a team (for example, alongside a family member) rather than going through the auction. This is one of the levers we use to keep team numbers balanced if registrations come in uneven.

Unsold players

Nobody is left behind

Step 1

Pass for now

If a player gets no bid at base, they're marked unsold and we move on — keeps the room moving.

Step 2

$5k re-auction round

After the main round, all unsold players go back up at a flat $5,000 base for a quick second round.

Step 3

Chit draw

Anyone still unsold is drawn by chit and assigned — but only to teams that still have a free slot and the budget. They join at the $5,000 base.

On the night

How to run the auction (console guide)

The auction console is the organiser's tool. It's password-protected and saves everything in your browser as you go.

1 · Open

Unlock the console

Open Auction console and enter the organiser password. Set the Squad target (7 or 8) at the top.

2 · Owners

Draw & seat the owners

As each owner draws their team chit, find them in the list and assign them to that team. They now show as that team's first player.

3 · Juniors

Place juniors (if needed)

Assign Virat and Dhruvan to their parents' teams.

4 · Nominate

Put a player on the block

Draw a chit, then click Block next to that player (or hit Next unsold). Their photo, positions and base price show in the spotlight.

5 · Bid

Take bids & sell

Watch each team's Left and Max bid on the boards. When sold, click the winning team's button in the spotlight and type the final price. It auto-advances to the next player.

6 · Unsold

Handle unsold

Leave passed players in the pool. After the main round, run the $5k re-auction, then the chit draw for any leftovers.

7 · Publish

Export the results

Click Export results to download results.js. Drop it into the season folder (and uncomment its line in index.html) — the Players & Teams page then shows the final rosters to everyone.

💡

Tips

• The console remembers everything even if the page reloads. • Use Copy JSON for a quick backup mid-auction. • Made a mistake? Click the × on a team board to send a player back to the pool.
